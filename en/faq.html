Getting started

Is CarLog free?

Yes, for one car with everything: service, fuel, documents, alerts, reports and backups. Premium is one purchase for people with more than one car, and adds automatic backups, PDF reports and car comparison. No ads either way.

Which phones does it run on?

Android phones from version 7 on, through Google Play. There’s no iPhone version.

Does it need the internet?

No. Everything in it works offline. It goes online through Google Play only to buy Premium and check the purchase, and to send a review if you write one.

Which languages and currencies?

Six languages: Arabic, English, French, Portuguese, Turkish and Malay. And 19 currencies, every Gulf currency among them, with kilometres or miles. Digits are Western in every language.

Service and fuel

How does it know when the next service is due?

Each type has an interval in kilometres, months or both, counted from the last time you logged it. Oil, for example, is every 5,000 km or six months, whichever comes first. Change it per car from the car’s ⋮ menu → “Service intervals”.

Why don’t I see fuel economy?

Economy is measured between two full tanks, so it needs at least two fill-ups marked “Full Tank”. If you forgot to log one in between, turn on “I missed logging a fill-up before this” on the next.

The garage did several things on one invoice. How do I log it?

As one visit with several items: “Add item” for each job, then “Price per item” or “One total”. The visit is counted once in the reports.

I log my fill-ups in another app. Do I start from zero?

No. In Settings → “Backup & Restore” → “Import from CSV”, import an export from Fuelio or Drivvo, or any spreadsheet with the date, odometer and quantity. You check them before they’re saved.

Documents and alerts

Why don’t alerts show in my notification shade?

That’s on purpose: CarLog doesn’t post notifications to the phone’s shade. Alerts are inside the app: the “Next alert” on the home screen, under each car, in “Notifications” (the bell), and on the home-screen widget.

When does it remind me about the registration and insurance?

30 days before the expiry date, then 7 days before. After expiry the alert stays, with the days since, until you renew the document.

Does it support Hijri dates?

Yes. In the document form, tap “Enter as Hijri” to enter the date in the Umm al-Qura calendar; every document date shows in both calendars.

Your data and backups

I changed phones. How do I move my data?

On the old phone: Settings → “Backup & Restore” → “Create Backup”, then send it to yourself. On the new one: “Restore from File”. If Google backup is on in your phone, your data usually moves by itself with your other apps.

I forgot my backup password.

It can’t be opened without it, by you or by us: its records and photos are encrypted with the password, and we keep no copy of it. If you still have the old phone, make a new backup from it.

Where are backups saved, and are they deleted with the app?

In Download/carLogBackup, and they stay after you uninstall the app, so you can restore if you install it again. On Android 9 and earlier they’re in the app’s folder and go with it, so send them to yourself first.

Is a backup without a password safe?

It’s obfuscated, not encrypted: any phone with CarLog can restore it. Keep it somewhere safe, or choose a password.

Premium

Is Premium a subscription?

No. It’s one purchase for life from Google Play, with nothing to renew. Google Play shows the price in your currency.

I bought Premium before. Do I pay again?

No. Open Premium and tap “Restore Purchases” while signed in with the Google account you bought it with. The same on a new phone.

What happens to my other cars without Premium?

They stay as they are and nothing is deleted, but they show as locked on the home screen, and the free version works with one car.

Privacy

Do you get my data?

No. We have no server that receives your records, there’s no account to sign up for, and the app has no ads or analytics. Your data is on your phone, and in Google’s backup of it if that’s on.

Then why the internet permission?

For Google Play only: buying Premium and checking it, and the review window. The app doesn’t ask for location, camera, contacts or notifications. The details are in the privacy policy.
