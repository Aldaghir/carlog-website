01Service

Every visit to the garage is a line in the book.

Pick what was done and its interval fills itself in: oil every 5,000 km or six months, the air filter every 20,000 km. If your dealer’s booklet says otherwise, change the interval for that car alone.

  • One visit, several itemsOil, filter and brake pads on one invoice, priced per item or as one amount, and counted once in the reports.
  • The receiptAttach a photo of the receipt to the record, instead of hunting for it in the glovebox.
  • BreakdownsMark an unplanned repair as a breakdown, and its frequency shows in the car’s health.
  • Your own itemsInjector cleaning or window tint: name it and set its interval.

02Reminders and documents

It reminds you before the date, not after.

Service is counted from the last odometer reading, documents from their expiry date. The home screen shows what’s due and what’s close, in plain words: “10,000 km overdue” or “expires in 19 days”.

  • RegistrationJust its number and expiry date (the Istimara in the Gulf). Reminders 30 days before, then 7 days before.
  • Inspection and insuranceWith the provider, the document number and the cost, if you like.
  • Hijri and GregorianEnter a date in either calendar, and see both.
  • On the home screenThe widget shows your car and its next date, with a one-tap fill-up button.

03Fuel

Log the fill-up before you close the fuel cap.

From the app icon’s shortcut or from the widget. Litres, price and cost: enter any two and the third is worked out.

Logging your fill-ups in Fuelio or Drivvo? Import them, or any spreadsheet with the date, odometer and quantity, and check them before they’re saved.

08Questions

Before you install it.

Does the app need the internet?

No, everything in it works offline. It goes online through Google Play only to buy Premium and check the purchase, and to send a review if you write one.

I changed phones. How do I move my data?

In Settings → Backup & Restore, create a backup and send it to yourself or save it, then use “Restore from File” on the new phone. If Google backup is on in your phone, your data usually moves by itself with your other apps.

I log my fill-ups in another app. Do I start from zero?

No. Import your fill-ups from Fuelio or Drivvo, or from any CSV file with the date, odometer and quantity. The app shows them to you before saving, and tells you what it couldn’t read.

I bought Premium before. Do I pay again?

No. Open Premium and tap “Restore Purchases” while signed in with the Google account you bought it with.
