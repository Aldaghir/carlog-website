Privacy policy

CarLog works on your phone. We don’t collect your data, and nothing you record in the app is sent to us.

Summary

  • Your cars, service, fuel and document records, and your settings are stored on your phone.
  • We have no servers that receive your data. The app has no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it doesn’t ask you to create an account.
  • The app goes online only through Google Play: to buy Premium and check the purchase, and to send a review if you choose to write one. Everything else works offline.
  • Your data leaves your phone only when you take an action, or through Google’s backup if it is turned on in your phone.

This policy covers the CarLog Android app (“the app”). “We” means its developer. If something here is unclear, write to android@aldaghir.com.

Data the app stores on your phone

The app keeps the following on your phone only:

  • Cars: make, model, year, colour, plate number, odometer reading, photo and notes.
  • Service: the date of each visit, odometer reading, items, cost, service centre, notes, receipt photo, the next service for each item, and breakdowns.
  • Fuel: date, odometer reading, amount, fuel type, cost, station and notes.
  • Documents: registration (Istimara), periodic inspection, insurance, driving licence and others, with their issue and expiry dates, provider, number, cost, notes and reminder.
  • Service intervals set for each car, and the reminders the app shows.
  • Photos: a copy of each photo you pick for a car or a receipt, in the app’s private storage. The original stays where it is.
  • Settings: language, theme, currency, distance unit, monthly budget, fingerprint lock and automatic backup.
  • Purchase status: whether you bought Premium, as Google Play reports it.
  • Review prompt: how many records you have saved and when the app last asked for a review, so it knows when to ask.
  • Backups: the ones you make, and the weekly automatic ones (Premium, the last five are kept). They are saved in your phone’s Downloads folder (Download/carLogBackup), or in the app’s folder on Android 9 and earlier.

What we don’t collect

We do not collect, receive, sell or share:

  • your name, email address, phone number or any account details;
  • your cars, records, documents or photos;
  • your location;
  • device identifiers or the advertising ID;
  • usage statistics or analytics.

The app contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting libraries. If you agreed in your phone’s settings to share usage and diagnostics data with Google, Google Play may show us aggregated crash reports that don’t identify you, under Google’s policy.

When data leaves your phone

Data leaves your phone only in these cases:

Purchases

CarLog Premium is bought through Google Play. Google handles the payment under the Google privacy policy. The app only receives the purchase status from Google Play and checks it from time to time. We never see your payment details.

In-app review

After you have saved five records, the app may ask for a review: at most once every 90 days, and three times in all. The review window is Google Play’s. If you write a review, Google Play sends it and publishes it under your Play account, under Google’s policy. The app sends none of your data with it.

Things you share or export

Backup files, CSV files and PDF reports are created on your phone. They go only where you choose to send or save them.

Things you import

A CSV file or a backup you pick in Android’s file picker is read on your phone only.

Links

The app’s website, this policy, the terms and the app’s Google Play page open in your browser or in the app you pick.

Google backup

If it is turned on in your phone, see the next section.

Android backup and moving to a new phone

If Google backup is turned on in your phone, Android copies CarLog’s data (your cars, records, photos and settings) to your Google account, under Google’s terms. We have no access to that copy. When you move to a new phone, by restoring from Google or with a direct phone-to-phone transfer, your data moves with you.

Your purchase status is not copied. The app restores it from Google Play when you use the same account you bought it with.

Google limits each app’s backup to 25 MB. If your data and photos are larger, they are not copied, so rely on the app’s own backup. You can turn Google backup off in your phone’s backup settings.

Security

  • App lock (optional): your fingerprint or your phone’s screen lock, through Android, so the app never sees your fingerprint or PIN. The lock comes back when the app has been in the background for more than a minute.
  • Widget: while the app is locked, the home-screen widget doesn’t show your car’s name or details.
  • Backups with a password: your records and photos in the backup are encrypted with AES-256-GCM, using a key derived from the password (PBKDF2), so only someone who knows it can open them.
  • Backups without a password: only obfuscated, and any phone with CarLog can restore them. The Downloads folder is open to your file manager and to any app you allowed to access your files, so protect a backup with a password if it holds anything that matters to you.

Permissions

The app never asks you for a permission. All of its permissions are the normal kind that Android grants at install:

PermissionWhy
Internet and network stateGoogle Play purchases and reviews only.
Google Play billingBuy CarLog Premium.
Biometrics and fingerprintUnlock the app with your fingerprint, if you turn the lock on.
Run at startup, wake lock and foreground serviceAdded by Android’s WorkManager library, so background tasks resume after your phone restarts: automatic backup, checking for service that is due, and checking the purchase.

The app does not ask for location, camera, storage, contacts, microphone, notifications or the advertising ID. You pick photos in Android’s photo picker, so the app needs no access to your gallery. Reminders appear inside the app only, so it sends nothing to your notification bar.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • Your data stays on your phone until you delete it.
  • “Delete all data” in Settings removes every car, with its records, documents and photos. Backup files are kept; you delete them in the Backup screen.
  • Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, removes everything the app stored in its own storage on your phone.
  • Backups in the Downloads folder stay after you uninstall the app, so you can restore them if you install it again. You delete them in the Backup screen or with your file manager. On Android 9 and earlier they are deleted with the app, because they are in its folder. Backups you sent or saved elsewhere stay where you put them.
  • Google’s backup of your phone is managed in your Google account, and your purchase history is kept by Google Play.

Because we hold none of your data, there is nothing for us to export or delete on our side.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect any data from children. The app doesn’t collect data from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If the app starts handling data differently, we will update this page and the date at the top before the change reaches you.

Governing law

This policy is governed by the laws of the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia. Saudi courts have jurisdiction over any dispute.
